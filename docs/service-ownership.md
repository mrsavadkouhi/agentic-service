Use the requested service and action together. Generic words such as “access”,
“group”, or “port” alone do not establish ownership. Mirza model access follows
the Mirza policy, directory-group updates belong to DWE, and network-port access
belongs to Network. Do not infer ownership of an arbitrary internal platform or
automation from the DWE list. Unspecified system-administration examples need
clear context; “and etc” is not permission to route every technical request to DWE.

Helpdesk is the default: unrelated or unclassified ordinary requests stay there
without ticket changes. Ambiguity does not justify moving a ticket to a guessed
group; local review may be recorded without external writes. Unsupported Mirza
matters still receive no external action under the Mirza allowlist rule.

Ordinary correction applies only to unassigned tickets currently in Helpdesk.
Preserve templates and human assignments. Ordinary requests already outside
Helpdesk remain untouched, even when their subject fits another support group.

transfer processes alone: their child tickets may intentionally belong to other
teams. Exclude those process tickets and associated children before ordinary
correction or Mirza enrollment; preserve their assignments and relationships.

messages for excluded tickets. A supported Mirza phrase in a process child does
not override this exclusion.

Identify actual process association from authoritative process/template and
parent/child metadata; exact live fields and IDs still need inspection. Do not
exclude every ticket with a parent, or rely only on an incidental lifecycle word.

request as an internal-transfer child must remain under its existing process.
